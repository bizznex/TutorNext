# Google contribution thesis — working draft

Status: **hypothesis for insider calibration, not an application claim**  
Prepared: 2026-07-17  
Decision deadline: Day 10

## One-sentence contribution

Identify expensive enterprise decisions that horizontal AI platforms do not solve by themselves, convert them into repeatable and governed Google Cloud-centered products, and lead the executive, technical, and commercial path from problem discovery to measurable adoption.

## Why this contribution could matter

Google already provides a broad enterprise agent foundation. Current official documentation describes ADK as a framework for building, evaluating, and deploying agents, with predictable workflow orchestration, tools, trajectories, and deployment to Agent Runtime, Cloud Run, or GKE. Gemini Enterprise adds permissions-aware enterprise data access, connectors, custom agents, and centralized agent use.

The proposed contribution therefore is not another generic agent framework. It is the product and transformation layer that makes the platform useful for a specific accountable buyer decision:

1. discover the costly decision and current failure;
2. define the evidence, business rules, human authority, and outcome;
3. prove a deterministic workflow before adding model autonomy;
4. use Google capabilities where they create a defensible customer and platform advantage;
5. quantify customer value, Google consumption, services pull-through, risk, and adoption;
6. turn validated work into reusable industry assets, offers, and executive narratives.

## Role-family hypothesis

The closest current contribution archetype is **Pursuit AI Lead / AI Transformation Lead**: executive problem discovery, AI services strategy, commercial pursuit, repeatable offers, adoption, and cross-functional orchestration. The current official Pursuit AI Lead listing is US-based and marked mid-level, so it validates the work pattern but not the target India/APAC team, level, or compensation.

Industry Solutions / Value Creation and a sufficiently senior Consulting Account Lead remain hypotheses, not confirmed targets. The workbook's earlier Consulting Account Lead URL now returns “job not found,” so it must not be presented as a current vacancy.

## What must be proven

- Two independent insiders name substantially the same team or role family for this contribution.
- A recruiter or hiring leader calibrates the realistic India/APAC level and identifies any hard experience gap.
- Historical evidence demonstrates executive problem discovery, productization, commercial accountability, implementation leadership, adoption, and personal decisions without confidential claims.
- A cloud architect confirms the required depth across data, IAM, runtime, evaluation, cost, scale, security, and hybrid integration.
- The four public products produce external evidence rather than architecture theatre or generic AI demos.
- Compensation feasibility is validated privately; no public document should claim an unverified package.

## Disqualifiers

- Applying before team, level, and evidence expectations are independently calibrated.
- Presenting the portfolio as four loosely connected demos instead of four evidence-gated products.
- Recreating capabilities already native to Google without a buyer-specific extension.
- Claiming confidential outcomes, unsupported savings, or employer-owned IP.
- Entering through a role that structurally resets scope below the intended senior contribution.
