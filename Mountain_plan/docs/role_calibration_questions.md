# Day 1 role-calibration pack

Use this as a conversation guide, not as a message to send verbatim. Ask for specific disagreement and evidence rather than encouragement.

## 30-second context

I am testing a contribution thesis: identify expensive enterprise decisions, turn them into governed and repeatable Google Cloud-centered products, and lead the executive, technical, and commercial path to adoption. I am building four public evidence products, but I do not yet know the correct Google team, level, or hiring bar. I need calibration, not a referral.

## Questions for Google insider 1

1. Which current Google Cloud team would most value this contribution: Pursuit/Consulting, Industry Solutions, Value Creation, Outbound Product, Customer Engineering, or another team?
2. What exact role title or internal role family should I investigate in India or APAC?
3. At what level would the scope be credible, and what would make that level unrealistic?
4. Which two pieces of evidence would make you personally comfortable introducing me to a named hiring leader?
5. Which parts of this thesis sound generic, duplicative of existing teams, or commercially weak?
6. How much hands-on architecture and delivery depth is tested for that role family?
7. What distinguishes an external transformation leader who succeeds at Google from one who fails after joining?
8. Who is a second person with a different vantage point who could independently challenge this answer?

## Questions for insider 2 or hiring leader

Ask the same core questions before revealing insider 1's answers. Independence matters for the Day 10 gate.

Add:

1. Does this profile read as a product leader, transformation/pursuit leader, consultant, or idea generator? Why?
2. Which historical outcomes must be quantified, and which product artifacts carry no hiring signal?
3. Would four public products clarify a repeatable contribution or dilute the flagship? What should be cut?
4. Is a Google-centered but Azure-interoperable position credible for the target team?
5. What is the correct private channel to calibrate India/APAC level and total compensation without anchoring on an unsupported number?

## Evidence capture

Record privately:

- interviewee role and vantage point;
- exact team/role/level named;
- two required evidence items;
- disqualifier or gap;
- architecture-depth expectation;
- willingness to introduce now: yes / no / only after gap closure;
- changed assumption and resulting action.

Publish only a consented, sanitized conclusion. Do not publish names, private compensation information, or verbatim notes.

## Day 1 completion test

- One targeted conversation completed or scheduled with a specific person.
- Written answers or a scheduled calendar commitment exist privately.
- At least one assumption in the contribution thesis is confirmed, rejected, or narrowed.
- The decision list is updated with a named owner and next action.
